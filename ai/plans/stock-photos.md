# Stock photo shortlist — interim imagery until Armak supplies real job photos

Researched 2026-10-11. **Nothing has been downloaded.** Every image below needs the user's approval before download.

Each candidate was checked on its live photo page or through Unsplash's own photo API:
- **Pexels:** the page shows a "Free" badge and the photo is covered by the Pexels License.
- **Unsplash:** `premium=false` and `plus=false`, so it is not Unsplash+, and the page shows "Download free". That means the standard Unsplash License applies.

I viewed every candidate as a thumbnail. Neither site lists file sizes, so the sizes below are estimates for the original file. Unsplash originals from 60 MP Sony bodies are very large. Download the "Large" (about 2400px) size or resize to about 2400px before committing.

Licensing notes:
- Neither license requires attribution, but a credit line is good practice.
- Neither license allows implying that the photographer or the people shown endorse Armak.
- Neither license allows selling the photos unaltered.

- **Unsplash `pl6My-v7gnE`** shows a large "NorthC" (data-center operator) label. Avoid.
- **Unsplash `SrIGkTxOjGE`** (retail) shows a MUJI storefront logo. Avoid.
- **Unsplash `VSam_So9b20` / `6tUsv-H35E8`** (Brock Wegner, cable pull from ceiling) are on-topic, but the fisheye and stylized look is too "influencer".
- **Pexels cottonbro server-room series** (e.g. 6804586) has a dark, moody, blue/purple cast. Off-palette.
- **Pexels 17636234** (IP phone in a data center) is heavy blue-tinted "cyber" light. Off-palette.
