1. **Confirm or remove the credential claims.** "Licensed · Bonded · Insured" and "BICSI-certified technicians" show on the homepage, /about/ and /services/ (`src/data/content.ts:84`). No license number is shown (`src/data/site.ts:26` is empty), and a public search of the California contractors board (CSLB) on 30 October found no license under "Armak". California generally requires a licensed contractor's license number in its advertising (general knowledge, not re-checked today). This is the item with real legal risk. Needs: your answers to questions 1 to 3.
2. **Protect the contact form's monthly allowance.** Web3Forms (the service that emails form messages to sales@armak.tech) stops taking messages after 250 in a month on its free plan, until the next month starts. Which plan Armak is on is not known (question 9). The form's key has to sit in the page, so anyone can copy it and send messages through it. If spam used up the 250, real customers would see "try again or call us" for the rest of the month. Today the form has a hidden spam trap and Web3Forms' own spam filter, but no "are you human" check. Needs: your answers to questions 9 to 12.
3. **Confirm the other claims visitors see:** 946+ projects, all 14 equipment brands (Hikvision included), five building types, "You get the reports" and "written plan and price". Needs: questions 4 to 8.
4. **Take the internal design page off the live site.** https://armak.tech/styleguide/ is a page for building the site. Google is told to skip it, but anyone with the address can open it, and it repeats "BICSI-certified" and "Licensed & bonded". A small code change (it was already listed as F13 in the 31 October code review). Needs: question 13.
5. **Finish the privacy page.** It doesn't say how long Armak keeps form emails, and it doesn't name the company that runs the sales@armak.tech inbox. Needs: questions 14 and 15, then two sentences on /privacy/.

7. **Give the form's Send a 15-second time limit.** Today, if Web3Forms never answers, the visitor sees "Sending…" until the browser gives up. With a limit, they would see the existing "try again or call us" message. A small code change in `src/components/forms/ContactForm.astro`.

Can wait: a street address on the site, a lawyer's look at /terms/ and at data agreements with vendors, real-visitor speed tracking, and the optional items marked in the tables.
