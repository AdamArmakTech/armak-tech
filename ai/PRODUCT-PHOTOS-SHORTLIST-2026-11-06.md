## Reuse rules by brand

**Ubiquiti.** Its terms say users may not "copy, reproduce, broadcast, transmit, republish, distribute, modify, prepare derivative works of, perform, publicly perform or display any Content in any way without the prior written permission of Ubiquiti and its applicable licensors." ([Terms of service](https://www.ui.com/legal/termsofservice/). The [EULA](https://ui.com/eula) has the same wording.) So Armak needs written permission first. No media library for resellers was found. Ubiquiti has a [UniFi Professional Integrators terms page](https://www.ui.com/legal/unifi/termsandconditions/) with brand rules, but no photo library was found there.

**Axis Communications.** Its guidelines PDF (section 2.1.2) says: "Axis does not permit the use of original Axis product pictures. Instead, we encourage and specifically authorize you to "deep link" to relevant product web pages on axis.com when marketing Axis products." It also says: "If you are an Axis partner, you are authorized to use the material available for you on the Axis Partner Pages, including product photos." ([Guidelines PDF](https://www.axis.com/dam/public/e9/35/3c/guidelines-for-using-axis-trademarks-and-copyrights-en-US-186188.pdf).) Partner library: Axis Partner Pages (www.axis.com/partner_pages/) and the A&E Media library ([aemedia.axis.com](https://aemedia.axis.com/)). Both need a partner login and were not opened.

**Cisco.** Its terms say: "You cannot post, modify, distribute, or reproduce in any way copyrighted or other proprietary materials" ([Terms](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/about/legal/terms-conditions.html)). Partner library: Cisco Brand Exchange ([bx.cisco.com](https://bx.cisco.com)), with brand assets and photos. It needs a Cisco partner login and was not opened.

**Crestron.** Section 5.3 says: "Photos in the "High-Res Photos & Logos" section of this website are permitted to be reproduced without Crestron's express prior written consent by the following parties: (a) Crestron Authorized Dealers; (b) Crestron Service Providers; (c) I2P and other authorized Partners; and (d) Members of the Media/Press." ([Website terms of use](https://www.crestron.com/Legal/Website-Terms-of-Use).) Partner library: the "High-Res Photos & Logos" section that the rule names. Its address was not found.
